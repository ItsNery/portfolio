Use case: ui-mockup. A complete desktop interactive personal website screen for Nery, landscape 1536x1024, edge to edge. It is an explorable game-like map, visually inspired by the clarity of a modern command-line interface, not a screenshot of an actual CLI application. The attached image is the outline of the state of Tlaxcala: the map should clearly evoke that silhouette. Render the world itself as minimal aligned character art and thin vector linework on an uninterrupted, flat dark forest-charcoal background. A tiny original stick-figure traveler stands at “Buenavista”. A dotted walking route physically connects markers for “Historia / Wikimedia”, “Proyectos”, “Conocimientos”, “Trayectoria” and “Contacto”. Give the map the vast majority of the screen and a strong composition, with abundant negative space: no filled landscape, no trees or villages drawn as pictures. A slim, quiet dialogue band integrated across the lower edge shows exactly “Nery: Hola, soy Nery. ¿Caminamos?” and an unobtrusive actionable “Elegir destino”. A narrow top line identifies “Nery / territorio” and offers clear controls “Mapa”, “Índice”, “Ayuda”. Use warm pale text, one restrained amber active marker; Poppins for normal Spanish interface labels, fixed-width typography only for the ASCII landscape. Let a few carefully chosen symbols evoke plant life and trail markers without excess. It is a working-feeling original interactive surface, not a poster or a marketing hero. No photographs, no textured paper, no simulated operating system chrome, no glass effects, no glow, no neon, no illustrated backgrounds, no fake code.
